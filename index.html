This portal is hosted directly on our Windows Server 2025 campus server backed by 1.8 TB of dedicated storage. Because everything runs locally on the campus network, students enjoy gigabit download speeds for study materials and zero-lag access to CTFd and Hackathon platforms without consuming external internet bandwidth.

  • Automated startup & self-healing services after server reboot
  • Direct LAN routing to CTFd competition environment
  • Centralized 1.8 TB repository for ISOs, datasets, and lecture archives
